My Experience

I participated in Harvard's CS50 Puzzle Day in 2025 and 2026, successfully solving the complete set of puzzles in both years. In 2025, I formed an international team with Etienne Guegan , a Global Engineering student at McGill University. We collaborated remotely through online calls, discussing different interpretations of the puzzles, developing solution strategies, and systematically checking our answers. Together, we successfully solved all 9 puzzles. In 2026, I returned to the competition as a solo participant and independently solved all 8 puzzles. Across the two years, the experience demonstrated both my international collaborative and independent analytical problem-solving abilities. The puzzles required me to break unfamiliar problems into smaller components, identify patterns and logical relationships, test alternative approaches, and verify solutions systematically. This progression from international teamwork to independent performance strengthened the analytical skills that really help in in the field of economics.
