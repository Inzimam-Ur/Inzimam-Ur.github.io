Erasmus+ Fully Funded Internship

Erasmus+ International Internship

European Union Mobility Opportunity through Ibn Haldun University

The Achievement

I was selected for an Erasmus+ internship opportunity through the international mobility program of the European Union and Ibn Haldun University. The opportunity was highly competitive, with only one internship position available for the entire Master's student body.

My selection was based on my academic performance, high CGPA, and selection/test results. Being selected for the single available position allowed me to undertake an international academic and professional experience in Poland.

01 University-Wide Position
2 Months Internship Duration
€1,200 Erasmus Funding
Poland Host Country

Acceptance from PUEB, Poland

Following the selection, I received an official acceptance from PUEB in Poland for a two-month internship. My internship journey taught me a lot, both academically and personally. Although visa issues prevented me from traveling and completing the internship in person, I was fortunate to have an excellent teacher and mentor who guided me throughout the experience. I gained valuable knowledge, skills, and professional confidence, and I am still connected with my mentor today.
