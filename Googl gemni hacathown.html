My Experience

I participated in the LabLab AI – Gemini Ultra 1.0 Hackathon, where I collaborated with a six-member international team to develop Anatomy Mentor AI, an AI-powered prototype designed to support anatomy students through interactive and adaptive learning. The project used the Gemini API to enable image recognition and analysis of anatomical images, generate customized multiple-choice questions, and provide interactive learning support. As the team's lead presenter, I was responsible for communicating the project's concept, technical innovation, educational value, and potential impact during the final evaluation. I delivered the final pitch and helped present how AI could transform traditional anatomy learning into a more interactive and personalized experience. Working with a six-member team also required close collaboration across different aspects of the project, including AI integration, real-time anatomical image processing, adaptive learning functionality, and the use of up-to-date medical information. The experience strengthened my skills in AI-assisted education, teamwork, technical communication, project presentation, and rapid problem-solving, while giving me practical experience in transforming a conceptual idea into a functional AI prototype.
