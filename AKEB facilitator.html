My Experience

From 2020 to 2023, I served as an Educational Facilitator and Mentor with the Aga Khan Educational Board Pakistan, volunteering during academic breaks to support students participating in Bridge Camp programs in Chitral, Pakistan.

Each summer, and during winter academic breaks, I contributed to educational activities designed to support students from underserved communities across Northern Pakistan. The Bridge Camp brought students together for intensive academic preparation, mentoring, personal development, and guidance to help them prepare for their future educational pathways.

My primary responsibility was to work with secondary-level students, providing academic instruction, mentoring, guidance, and support throughout the camp.
