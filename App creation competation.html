My Experience

I participated in the LabLab AI – GPT-4 Powered App Creation & Evals Hackathon , where I collaborated with a four-member international team to develop InterviewPrepGPT, an AI-powered application designed to help users prepare for job interviews. The application analyzes a user's resume and job description and generates relevant interview questions based on the information provided. Users can then answer the generated questions as thoroughly as possible and receive AI-powered feedback designed to identify weaknesses, highlight shortfalls, and help them improve their interview preparation. The project used GPT-3.5 API calls to analyze resumes and job descriptions, generate interview questions, and provide personalized feedback. We carefully designed and tailored the prompts to produce useful and context-specific results for users rather than relying only on generic interview questions. The experience gave me practical exposure to generative AI, prompt engineering, API integration, collaborative development, and AI-assisted career preparation. We also continued working on improvements to the application with the goal of developing future versions and expanding its functionality.

InterviewPrepGPT

InterviewPrepGPT is an AI-powered interview preparation tool that combines a user's resume with a target job description to generate personalized interview questions. The application allows users to respond to these questions and receive detailed AI-generated feedback that helps identify areas for improvement and prepare more effectively for real interviews.
