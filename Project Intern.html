Project Intern – Educational Programs

Education, Field Research & Project Management

My Experience

I completed a two-month Project Internship with the Aga Khan Education Board (AKEB) from June to August 2023 through the Global Encounter program.

I was selected as one of only two interns from across Pakistan for this internship opportunity. The placement provided exposure to educational program delivery, fieldwork, project monitoring, teaching, data collection, and program evaluation .

I initially worked in Chitral, where I supported the implementation and monitoring of an educational bridge camp involving more than 200 participants.

During the field placement, I supported the project team with the administration and delivery of the program, monitoring activities and participant engagement, supporting project leads, and teaching selected courses .

I also collected and organized information from the field to help assess the effectiveness of the program. This provided practical experience in connecting field-level observations with structured program evaluation .

Following the fieldwork, I travelled to Karachi to work with the Aga Khan Education Board team. I consolidated and analyzed the information collected during the field project, prepared an evaluation report, and presented my findings and recommendations to the relevant project stakeholders.

This experience allowed me to work across both field and organizational settings, connecting on-the-ground educational program implementation with data-based evaluation, reporting, and project management .

Field Project – Chitral

The field component of the internship took place in Chitral, where I worked directly with the educational bridge camp and its participants.

My responsibilities included supporting the administration and delivery of educational activities, monitoring program performance, supporting project leads, and teaching selected courses .

I also observed program activities and collected information that could be used to evaluate the effectiveness of the project.

The field experience gave me first-hand exposure to the challenges involved in delivering educational programs in a local community setting. It also helped me understand the importance of coordination, participant engagement, monitoring, and evidence-based program improvement .

Evaluation & Reporting

After completing the fieldwork, I moved to Karachi, where I worked with the Aga Khan Education Board team for the remaining part of the internship.

I consolidated the information collected during the field project and contributed to the evaluation of the educational bridge camp.

Based on the data, observations, and information collected during the fieldwork, I prepared an evaluation report highlighting key findings, observations, and recommendations for improving future program implementation.

I also presented the findings and recommendations to relevant team members, connecting field-level experience with structured reporting and evidence-based program improvement .

Organization

The placement gave me the opportunity to experience both field-level educational programming and organizational-level project evaluation , bridging practical community work with structured reporting and program improvement.
