My Experience

I was selected as a Section Leader for Stanford University's Code in Place , a global volunteer education initiative that brings Stanford's introductory computer science curriculum to learners around the world. Selected from thousands of applicants , I contributed as a volunteer instructor and mentor, supporting students from diverse countries, age groups, academic backgrounds, and professional experiences as they learned programming and computational problem-solving.

Throughout the program, I conducted 40+ individual one-on-one sessions , with each session lasting approximately one hour , helping students understand programming concepts, debug their code, work through challenging problems, and develop confidence in approaching computational tasks independently.

This experience strengthened my ability to explain technical concepts clearly to learners with very different levels of prior experience while also developing my own communication, mentoring, Python, and computational problem-solving skills. More importantly, it reinforced my commitment to teaching and knowledge sharing — an important part of my long-term goal of pursuing an academic career in economics that combines rigorous research with dedicated teaching and mentorship.
