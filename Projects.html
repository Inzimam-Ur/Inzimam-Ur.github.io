My Projects

This section presents selected projects completed during my academic and professional journey. My work covers financial economics, sustainable development, fintech, social finance, econometrics, data analysis, and technology .

These projects have allowed me to combine economic theory with data analysis, statistical methods, econometric modeling, natural language processing, and applied research to investigate real-world problems.

Featured Projects

01 / 04

SDGs and Crowdfunding Success

Master's Thesis

Investigated how alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals influences the success of online crowdfunding campaigns.

The study analyzed 138,393 Kickstarter campaigns from 2020–2024 using Natural Language Processing, Python, R, and econometric techniques.

02 / 04

Charity-Based Financing in the Era of Technology

Literature Review

Conducted a systematic literature review examining the role of financial technology in charitable fundraising and social finance.

The study reviewed 112 peer-reviewed articles published between 2010 and 2024 using evidence from Scopus and Web of Science.

FinTech Social Finance Scopus Research

03 / 04

R&D, Trade Openness & Innovation

Econometric Analysis

Examined the relationship between research and development expenditure, trade openness, and innovation performance.

The project used panel data from seven OECD countries covering 1991–2009 and applied Autoregressive Distributed Lag (ARDL) modeling.

ARDL Panel Data Econometrics OECD

04 / 04

FinTech & Individual Donation Behavior

Survey & Data Analysis

Developed and validated a measurement scale to examine how FinTech tools influence individual donation behavior in Türkiye and Pakistan.

Using primary survey data and Exploratory Factor Analysis, the project identified key behavioral dimensions including perceived usefulness, attitudes, behavioral intention, and perceived behavioral control.
