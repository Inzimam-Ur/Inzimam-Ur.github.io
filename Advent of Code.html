My Experience

I participated in Advent of Code, an international programming and problem-solving competition that attracts thousands of participants from students and professional programmers around the world. The competition runs for 25 days, with two progressively challenging programming problems released each day, requiring participants to develop solutions ranging from basic computational logic to more advanced algorithmic reasoning. When I began the competition, I initially struggled with the increasing complexity of the problems and found it difficult to consistently complete the daily challenges. Rather than abandoning the competition, I adopted a continuous learning approach: I experimented with different solution strategies, debugged unsuccessful attempts, consulted friends when necessary, and researched unfamiliar concepts and approaches through online resources. Through this iterative process, I progressively improved my ability to understand unfamiliar problems, decompose them into manageable components, design algorithms, test solutions, and learn from failed attempts. I ultimately completed all 50 problems, demonstrating both consistency and persistence throughout the full 25-day challenge.
