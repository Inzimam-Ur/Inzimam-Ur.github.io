Global Encounters Camp 2026

International Leadership & Facilitation

Selected as one of only two Pakistani facilitators for Global Encounters Camp 2026 in Kenya

The Achievement

I was selected as one of only two Pakistani facilitators for Global Encounters Camp 2026 after applying for the program for three consecutive years. Having been unsuccessful during the interview stage in my first two applications, I continued to apply, learn, and improve. In 2026, I was finally selected for the facilitator role and given the opportunity to travel to Kenya.

The selection was particularly meaningful because the program attracts thousands of applicants from across Pakistan. Being chosen as one of only two Pakistani facilitators allowed me to represent Pakistan in an international youth leadership environment and work alongside young people from diverse cultural, geographical, and social backgrounds.

Three Years of Persistence

This journey took three years. I applied to Global Encounters Camp three times. During my first two applications, I reached the interview stage but was not selected. Instead of giving up, I treated each attempt as an opportunity to learn, improve my communication and leadership skills, and prepare myself better for the next opportunity.

On my third application, I was selected as a facilitator for Global Encounters Camp 2026 in Kenya. This experience taught me that persistence, continuous learning, and the willingness to improve can eventually turn repeated rejection into an opportunity.

International Youth Leadership

At the camp, I worked with 67+ young people from more than 19 countries, bringing together participants from regions including Europe, Asia, Africa, and Australia. The diversity of perspectives made the experience a unique international learning environment.

  • Facilitated: Supported and guided youth throughout the camp activities and learning experiences.
  • Mentored: Helped young participants develop confidence, leadership abilities, and teamwork skills.
  • Led: Worked with an international group of young people and contributed to creating an inclusive and collaborative environment.
  • Learned: Gained new perspectives from young people and fellow facilitators representing different cultures and parts of the world.

What I Learned

The Global Encounters experience taught me that leadership is not only about teaching others, but also about learning from them. Working with young people from different countries challenged me to communicate across cultures, listen to different perspectives, adapt to new situations, and lead with empathy.

I entered the camp as a facilitator, but I also left as a learner. The experience strengthened my leadership, communication, mentoring, teamwork, and cross-cultural collaboration skills and gave me a much broader understanding of how young people from different parts of the world think, learn, and work together.
