Bachelor's Degree Scholarships

International Islamic University Islamabad

Merit-Based Tuition Award & Fully Funded Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship

The Achievement

During my Bachelor's degree in Economics at International Islamic University Islamabad, my academic performance earned me two merit-based scholarship awards at different stages of my undergraduate studies.

In my second semester, I achieved a 3.90 GPA, the highest GPA in my class for that semester. As a result, I received a 100% tuition fee award for one semester, awarded to the top three students in the class based on academic merit.

Building on my academic performance, I was subsequently selected for the Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship, a merit- and need-based scholarship that supported my full Bachelor's degree. In addition to covering tuition, the scholarship provided a stipend to support my educational expenses throughout my undergraduate studies.

These scholarships recognized both my academic performance and my potential to succeed in higher education, enabling me to pursue my Bachelor's degree with substantial financial support while maintaining a strong academic record.

Scholarship Recognition

The first award was a merit-based one-semester tuition scholarship earned after achieving the highest GPA in my class during my second semester. The award covered 100% of tuition for that semester and was given to the top three students in the class.

The second award was the Ehsaas Undergraduate Scholarship, which provided financial support for my entire Bachelor's degree. In addition to tuition support, the scholarship included a stipend to help cover educational and living expenses.

Awarding Institution: International Islamic University Islamabad

International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI) is a public-sector university in Pakistan offering undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral education across a wide range of disciplines, including economics and social sciences.
