My Experience

Following my undergraduate graduation, I secured an internship opportunity with U Microfinance Bank Limited through a professional connection I developed on LinkedIn.

I connected with the Islamabad Area Manager, who recognized my academic background and offered me an internship opportunity with the possibility of further employment based on my performance.

I completed a two-month internship from February to April 2023, working at one of the bank's premium branches in Blue Area, Islamabad.

The experience gave me direct exposure to the microfinance banking sector, financial-services operations, administration, customer-oriented workflows, and professional banking environments .

Working within a professional branch environment allowed me to understand how financial institutions manage their day-to-day operations and how banking services are delivered to customers. I also developed practical experience with computer-based administrative tasks, organizational coordination, and professional communication .
