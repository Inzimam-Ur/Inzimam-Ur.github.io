Graduate Teaching Fellow – Statistics

Teaching, Quantitative Methods & Research

My Experience

I have served as a Graduate Teaching Fellow in Statistics at Ibn Haldun University since September 2023. In this role, I support undergraduate statistics education by leading a weekly section and helping students develop a practical understanding of statistical methods and quantitative analysis.

I teach and facilitate discussions on statistical concepts, quantitative methods, regression analysis, ANOVA, time-series analysis, and data visualization . My teaching emphasizes the application of statistical techniques to economics, finance, and real-world datasets rather than focusing only on theoretical concepts.

As part of the teaching fellowship, I work with 120+ undergraduate students, guiding them through practical exercises and helping them interpret statistical results and communicate data-driven findings. I regularly work with Stata, SPSS, Excel, Python, and R for teaching, demonstrations, and quantitative analysis.

In addition to teaching responsibilities, I support the course professor with academic and research-related tasks, including literature reviews, financial-data analysis, data preparation, and quantitative analysis . This combination of teaching and research assistance has strengthened my ability to communicate quantitative concepts, analyze empirical data, and work effectively in an academic research environment.
