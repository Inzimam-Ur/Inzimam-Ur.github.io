Full Master's Scholarship & Graduate Teaching Fellowship

Ibn Haldun University

Merit-Based Full Tuition Scholarship & Graduate Teaching Fellowship

The Achievement

I was awarded a merit-based full tuition scholarship to pursue my Master's degree at Ibn Haldun University in Istanbul, Türkiye. The scholarship covered the complete tuition cost of my two-year Master's program in Financial Economics, recognizing my academic performance and potential for advanced study.

In addition to the scholarship, I was awarded a Graduate Teaching Fellowship in Statistics, a highly selective academic opportunity awarded to only one student each year. The fellowship was valued at approximately ₺400,000 and recognized my academic strength, quantitative abilities, and potential to contribute to teaching and academic research.
