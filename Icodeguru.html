My Experience

Since September 2025, I have been working remotely as a Teacher with iCodeGuru, providing free online education to students from underserved communities in Pakistan.

My teaching focuses on TOEFL preparation and Python programming , with the goal of making high-quality academic and technical learning opportunities accessible to students who may face financial barriers to education.

As part of this role, I delivered a one-month free online live TOEFL preparation course, helping students develop their English language skills, test-taking strategies, and confidence for the TOEFL examination.

I also contribute to Python programming education, supporting students in developing foundational programming and problem-solving skills.

Beyond live teaching, I design course outlines, presentation slides, learning materials, exercises, and structured lesson content to ensure that students receive organized and practical learning experiences.

The experience combines my interests in education, technology, programming, English language learning, and community service , while allowing me to support students who otherwise may not have access to paid educational resources.
